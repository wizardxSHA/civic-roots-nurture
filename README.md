# Civic Roots Foundation

**Empowering BIPOC and Underrepresented Youth Through Civic Engagement and Leadership**

---

## Who We Are

Civic Roots Foundation is a BIPOC-led, youth-centered organization committed to empowering BIPOC and underrepresented youth to shape the civic and diplomatic future of Canada. Rooted in Atlantic Canada, we build pathways for young people to engage in leadership, policy, and cultural exchange — strengthening representation, belonging, and voice in every space where decisions are made.

## Our Mission

To empower youth to lead with purpose, advocate for equity, and build bridges across cultures through civic participation and diplomacy.

## Our Vision

A Canada where every young person — regardless of race, background, or region — has equitable access to civic leadership, representation, and opportunity.

## Our Values

- **Equity**: Centering marginalized and underrepresented voices
- **Empowerment**: Building leadership and confidence through civic action
- **Community**: Strengthening belonging through collaboration and mentorship
- **Representation**: Promoting diversity in governance, policy, and diplomacy

## What We Do

### Youth Civic Leadership Program
Training BIPOC and underrepresented youth in civic engagement, leadership, and community organizing. Participants gain skills in public speaking, policy literacy, and community impact.

### Atlantic Youth Dialogue
A regional forum bringing together youth from across Atlantic Canada to discuss equity, representation, and community development through collaboration and shared advocacy.

### Cultural Diplomacy & Global Exchange
Connecting Canadian youth with international platforms — including delegations, conferences, and global youth assemblies — to strengthen cross-cultural understanding and amplify Canada's diverse voices abroad.

### Mentorship & Storytelling Lab
A creative space where youth share their lived experiences through art, writing, and digital storytelling, building confidence, public presence, and advocacy through narrative leadership.

## Our Impact

We are building a generation of youth who lead with empathy, equity, and action. Through training, dialogue, and global engagement, our programs support young leaders in shaping community projects, policy discussions, and international representation.
